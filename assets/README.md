## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished square app icon for a Home Assistant add-on named localstar-openport. Primary subject: a bold five-point golden star with a round nautical ship porthole built into its center. The porthole has a simple substantial metal rim with four understated rivets, dark blue edging, and blue-teal glass with a restrained highlight. The star silhouette must remain unmistakable. Style: clean, friendly, crisp modern illustrated app mark, mostly flat color with very subtle dimensional shading, thick simple forms and excellent readability at 128x128 and 48x48. Composition: one centered symmetrical mark, one point straight up, comfortable consistent transparent padding all around, star occupying about 85% of square. Genuine transparent background outside the mark, no tile or solid backdrop. No words, letters, numbers, branding text, watermark, extra stars, clouds, scenery, or mockup. Deliver a single finished icon, not a sheet of variants.
